# Research: MDF-e Source and Schema Baseline

**Consulted**: 2026-10-12
**Feature**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Decisions

### Decision: Keep legal, technical, and operational authority separate

**Decision**: Use CONFAZ acts as normative legal authority; use the live SVRS MDF-e documents index for the current technical/manual/schema listing; use the SVRS services catalog for service routes; use Sao Paulo Fazenda pages only for state-specific context. Label historical indexes, navigation pages, and secondary sources separately.

**Rationale**: These sources establish different facts. A service listing does not define fiscal obligations, while a normative act does not provide the current WSDL route or prove deployment of a schema revision.

**Alternatives considered**: Treating the SPED/RFB download page as the sole current source was rejected because its MDF-e listing shows MOC 3.00a while the live SVRS index shows MOC 3.00b. Treating captured PR/PB pages or secondary libraries as normative was rejected by the repository constitution and feature requirements.

### Decision: Select the live SVRS listing as a candidate technical baseline, not a complete applicability decision

**Decision**: Record MOC 3.00b and the SVRS-listed `PL_MDFe_300b_NT012025_1.04.zip` package as the current technical-index candidates. Do not claim that revision 1.04 is applicable to every operation, UF, or date until the package contents and official rollout/applicability material are reconciled.

**Rationale**: The official SVRS documents index lists the MOC and package, including the 25 Apr 2026 revision 1.04 entry. It does not provide a complete per-revision effective interval or deployment matrix. The package must be downloaded from its official link, hashed, inspected for namespaces/schema families, and compared with the relevant NT before generation is planned.

**Alternatives considered**: Selecting the older MOC 3.00a entry as current was rejected because the live SVRS index and the current gov.br MDF-e route point to the newer 3.00b source. Assuming the newest package applies universally was also rejected because the official listing does not establish that fact.

### Decision: Keep SP as a candidate cohort and keep contingency outside the initial runtime claim

**Decision**: Carry SP forward as the initial candidate in the scope matrix, but require primary evidence for issuer eligibility, accreditation, each operation, and environment before promoting the dependent runtime spec. Mark contingency as unverified and excluded unless an official source establishes the allowed mode, activation, routing, and recovery.

**Rationale**: Sao Paulo's official overview says MDF-e is authorized nationally by SVRS and links to the national portal. The reviewed pages do not establish a complete SP issuer/operation matrix or a MDF-e contingency procedure.

**Alternatives considered**: Inferring full SP support from the national SVRS catalog was rejected because the catalog is not a complete issuer-eligibility or per-UF matrix. Including contingency based on unrelated NF-e/NFC-e behavior or a status/availability notice was rejected because neither establishes MDF-e contingency rules.

### Decision: Do not add XSDs or generated declarations in this feature

**Decision**: Limit this feature to evidence and design artifacts. The follow-on schema/runtime work may add official XSDs only after provenance, package identity, namespace, applicability, and generation ownership have been verified.

**Rationale**: The repository currently has no dedicated MDF-e XSD family. Existing incidental `chMDFe` fields in Suframa event schemas do not constitute MDF-e support. The current generator writes recursive index files even when filtered and skips existing generated files while recording collisions; running it before understanding those effects risks unrelated package changes.

**Alternatives considered**: Running `npm run generate` against an unverified archive was rejected because it would turn an unresolved source choice into generated package output and could affect unrelated indexes.

### Legal and Technical Applicability

- The consolidated Ajuste 21/10 says clause 2's qualified electronic signature wording has applied since 1 Sep 2022 (Ajuste 23/22), and clause 3's current emission timing wording has applied since 13 Dec 2023 (Ajuste 45/23). The unloading-UF rule changed under Ajuste 27/25 from 9 Oct 2025 through 31 May 2026, then under Ajuste 05/26 from 1 Jun 2026. The currently published wording distinguishes documents by unloading UF.
- Ajuste 03/26's CIOT legal effect starts 1 Jun 2026. The SVRS page separately lists NT 2026.001 v1.00 with homologation scheduled 21 Sep 2026 and production 23 Nov 2026. As of consultation, the homologation date had passed and production date was future; actual rollout, schema-package linkage, and enforcement are not verified.
- The SVRS page lists NT 2025.001 package revision 1.04 on 25 Apr 2026 and says that revision includes `procEmi` value 4 for PAA. The NT page separately notes a 6 Oct 2025 production date for NT 2025.001; that does not establish the rollout interval for archive revision 1.04.
- MOC 3.00b and the listed base package are current-index candidates. The legacy RFB/SPED MOC 3.00a listing is historical/conflicting. No source reviewed establishes a complete package-by-date, operation, environment, and UF applicability interval.

### SP Issuer Cohort and Operational Delta

Fazenda SP says MDF-e authorization is national through SVRS and directs issuers to service URLs, not pinned IP addresses (SRC-007). Portaria CAT 102/2013 article 1 §1 describes issuers as taxpayers accredited by Fazenda SP as NF-e or CT-e issuers (SRC-011). Article 2 ties emission to CT-e issuance or to NF-e transport profiles, including own/leased vehicles or contracted autonomous carriers; the consolidated state rules contain later amendments, so this is only a candidate cohort, not an exhaustive eligibility determination.

Portaria SRE 22/2026 is an explicit current state delta: from 1 Jun 2026, §2 uses one MDF-e per unloading UF, and §2-A permits multiple MDF-e per unloading UF for mixed own/third-party cargo or TAC documents issued by different contractors (SRC-012). This legal change does not establish that the matching SVRS schema revision is deployed. No reviewed primary source completes the per-issuer accreditation/permission matrix or proves environment-specific access for the candidate SP cohort.

## Repository Findings

- The repository has no dedicated MDF-e schema package under `xsd/` and no MDF-e runtime facade. Two incidental `chMDFe` fields exist in Suframa event XSDs and their generated companions; they are not an MDF-e manifest model.
- The normal generator output is split among `src/classes/`, `src/interfaces/`, `src/enums/`, and `src/types/`, with indexes and `src/manifest.json`.
- `tools/generate.ts` regenerates indexes recursively after filtered generation. It does not replace an existing generated module; differing generated output is reported as a collision. The baseline feature must not execute generation, and a later codegen plan must address these behaviors and prove unrelated output remains unchanged.
- `specs/031-mdfe-sp-runtime/spec.md` explicitly depends on this baseline and already treats production routing, issuer prerequisites, schema applicability, and contingency as gates. This research makes no promise beyond those gates.
